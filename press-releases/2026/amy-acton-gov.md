# Dr. Amy Acton’s chance to reshape North American industry

Congratulations to Dr Amy Acton for becoming the first elected female governor of Ohio after a fiercely contested election.

Policy issues during the election campaign of Dr Acton focused on economy,data centers,energy and cost of living. These issues have been common in campaigns of candidates in other states too and have been a feature of 2026 gubernatorial elections in general. AI has now become a major political issue in the USA and so has energy. 

The stand taken by Dr Acton regarding regulation of data centers and her promises regarding worker protection and wage raise resonated with the people of Ohio. 

But what was not apparent in the election is how important Ohio is to the USA. Ohio is the birthplace of some of the biggest corporations in the world. Procter and Gamble,Kroger,Wendy's, Goodyear,Standard Oil, Owens Corning and more representing health,finance,energy and engineering 

These corporations are supported by an extremely well developed research base through organisations Ohio Aerospace Institute (OAI), University of Dayton Research Institute (UDRI),NASA Glenn Research Center and research centers in Ohio state and Cincinnati Universities. 

Non profit organizations like ASM international headquartered in Ohio represent one of the biggest associations of materials scientists and engineers in the world. 

It is not an exaggeration to say that Ohio is a research and industrial powerhouse. While it has nothing to do with local elections, it puts Ohio in  a very strong geopolitical position.

Ohio is landlocked and will benefit immensely from access to free waters. Maine becomes important strategically and together with Canadian provinces Ontario,Newfoundland and Quebec and US states New York ,Pennsylvania, New Jersey,Connecticut ,Massachusetts— Ohio could build a new industrial powerhouse around the North East American states. There is a real opportunity here. 

Dr Acton possesses the most overwhelming advantage in the North American continent. Federal oversight and regulations will expect her to work within the bounds of a state governor. But the world today demands much more. 

Ohio could play it safe and do more of what it's already been doing. That's fine and maybe prudent too. But other states including the provinces in Canada may not share the same conservative attitude. 

Their ambition to grow their economies is not a secret any more. Ohio could help them and gain significant advantages that would protect its long term interests and put it in a position from where it can influence the world and play a very significant role in its development. 

Leaders in Canada had been extremely vocal about some of the issues with regards to trade with the US. The federal government could not arrive at an agreeable solution. Premier Doug Ford was quite candid about his frustration with the US federal government.This is the opportunity that he wanted. A new leadership in Ohio could offer a way to reset relations. It's time to act. 

I wish Dr Amy Acton the best as she prepares to take on the responsibility of the Governor in 2027 and look forward to the changes that she will bring to transform Ohio and help it realise its full potential.
